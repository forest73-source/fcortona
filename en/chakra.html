Crypto Art · NFT on Ethereum

7 CHAKRACOLLECTION

✦

In 2021 Cortona entered the world of Crypto Art, creating the NFT collection ‘7CHAKRA COLLECTION’ on the Ethereum blockchain. A special project with a Humanitarian purpose that combines the artistic expression of traditional painting with new technological frontiers.

  • 33unique works
  • EthereumBlockchain
  • ♥Royalties to charity

The Project

The original works are painted with luminescent pigments that glow in the dark: each one embodies the energy of a chakra and is conceived as a tool for mindfulness. Inspired by the yogi master Sadhguru, the project was born with the mission of spreading balance, serenity and light.

The royalties from each work go to a charity chosen by the first buyer, and part of the proceeds is donated to the Isha Foundation.
