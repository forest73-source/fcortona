Introduction

The NFT project 7CHAKRA Collection was born from the desire to share awareness and knowledge through art, fostering spiritual growth and supporting humanitarian initiatives. Through blockchain technology and NFTs (Non-Fungible Tokens), the project aims to promote equality, balance and well-being both for those who take part directly and for those who benefit from donations to non-governmental organisations (NGOs).

The project also offers buyers a unique opportunity to experience art and spirituality in a tangible and personal way: in addition to the digital artworks in the collection, they will have access to exclusive benefits that enrich their experience and their relationship with the works themselves.

Vision

The vision behind the project is to use art as a means to foster spiritual growth and support humanitarian causes. Through unique and engaging artworks, the project aims to inspire and raise awareness of spiritual themes, drawing on the teachings of Sadhguru, the Indian yogi and mystic.

Goals

  • Fostering spiritual growth

    Through artworks and related content, the project aims to share spiritual knowledge and practices to support individual and collective growth.

  • Supporting humanitarian causes

    Part of the proceeds from the sale of the works will be donated to non-governmental organisations chosen by the buyer, providing ongoing support to those in need.

  • Transparency through blockchain

    By using the Ethereum blockchain, the project ensures transparent and traceable transactions, guaranteeing that donations reach their intended destinations.

Project Features

  • Unique artworks

    The collection includes digital and physical works: 33 pieces, 9 of them physical, each associated with a different Chakra, made in mixed media on card with luminescent pigments that glow in the dark.

  • NGO involvement

    Buyers can choose which non-governmental organisation to support with part of the proceeds from the sale of the works, ensuring a direct impact on the community.

  • Transparency and traceability

    Every transaction is recorded on the blockchain and made public, allowing buyers to follow the path of their contributions and ensuring that donations reach their intended destinations.

  • Purchase with cryptocurrency

    The works can be purchased with cryptocurrency, offering buyers greater flexibility and ease of access.

  • Related content

    Beyond the artworks, the project offers additional content such as spiritual practices, useful links and information on the themes explored, helping to expand awareness and knowledge.

Exclusive Benefits for Buyers

Exclusive benefit

Unique and Meaningful Jewels

The 7 Chakra Miniatures

The works of the 7 Chakra NFT collection are not only digital pieces, but also 7 miniatures hand-painted on sheet metal, each representing a chakra. These physical artworks are made with oil and photoluminescent pigment, giving them a unique luminosity and beauty.

The 7 miniatures in the dark: the photoluminescent pigment lights up. Each piece measures 5 × 3.5 cm.

  • A unique sensory experience

    Thanks to the photoluminescent pigment, the works reveal themselves in the dark, creating a unique and immersive sensory experience that lets you fully appreciate their beauty and meaning in moments of calm and reflection.

  • To be worn as jewellery

    Each miniature is designed to be worn: its compact size (5 × 3.5 cm) makes it perfect as a pendant, so you can carry your spiritual and artistic connection with you wherever you go.

  • Representing the 7 Chakras

    Each pendant represents one of the seven main chakras, in its corresponding colour, to express one's interest in spirituality and inner well-being in a discreet and meaningful way.

  • Handcrafted

    Each piece is made by hand by the artist, with an attention to detail that makes every one unique and precious.

  • Connection with the digital collection

    Buying a pendant also gives access to the corresponding work in the NFT collection: the tangible experience of the jewel together with the digital one on the blockchain.

  • Contribution to philanthropy

    Part of the proceeds from the sale of the jewels will be donated to non-governmental organisations chosen by the buyer, directly supporting humanitarian causes.

Exclusive Auction

The 9 Physical Works

The physical works of the 7 Chakra NFT collection are 9 pieces painted with photoluminescent pigments in mixed media on card, 32 × 42 cm: unique and rare works, a precious addition to any art collection.

  • A tangible connection with art

    A physical work allows a concrete connection with art and spirituality, to be lived and appreciated in the real world.

  • Exclusive access to the auction

    The physical works will be auctioned exclusively to those who bought the first NFT of the collection, with the chance to win a unique piece for their own collection.

  • Contribution to philanthropy

    Part of the auction proceeds will be donated to non-governmental organisations chosen by the buyer, providing further support to humanitarian causes.

How to Buy

The works are available for purchase on OpenSea, the NFT and crypto-art marketplace. Buyers can select the works they want and complete the transaction using cryptocurrency. Once the purchase is made, they can choose the non-governmental organisation that will receive part of the proceeds.

A percentage of the royalties generated by each work will be donated to the non-governmental organisation chosen by the buyer. This cycle of perpetual donations ensures a continuous and meaningful impact on the beneficiary communities.

Conclusion

The 7 Chakra Collection NFT project is a unique opportunity to combine art, spirituality and humanitarian action. By sharing unique artworks and spiritual practices and supporting non-governmental organisations, the project aims to promote a vision of equality, balance and well-being for all.
