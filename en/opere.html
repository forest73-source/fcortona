Series I

Innocence

2015 · 17 oils on canvas

✦

The debut series INNOCENCE I (17 oils on canvas) marks a fundamental turning point in Cortona's artistic research. Nourished by a dialogue with the great masters of photography, Italian and international, through a deep experimentation with primary colours and their visceral nuances, an intense visual and emotional metamorphosis takes shape.

From the partial framings of the early works, the artist's gaze gradually narrows until it “enters” the subjects themselves, breaking down every distance: the space on the canvas shrinks to make room for the exploration of the Soul. An inner immersion that deepens further in series II and III, turning painting into a pure journey through the emotional depth of humanity.

Series II

Innocence

2017 · Oil and photoluminescent pigments on galvanized sheet metal

✦

INNOCENCE – Series II stems from Cortona's research into inner light and from the desire to give new life to her subjects' gaze through art.

The works are handmade on galvanized sheet metal with natural photoluminescent pigments which, once charged by light, glow in the dark, revealing intense and surprising colours. Each painting is the result of a long artisanal process, in which technique and sensitivity merge to create a constantly changing visual experience.

Light becomes an integral part of the work, revealing what remains invisible and inviting the viewer to embark on a journey towards discovering their own inner light.

Series III

Innocence

2019 · 10 oils on galvanized sheet metal — black and white

✦

Series III of INNOCENCE is the final chapter of the series, made up of 10 oil paintings on galvanized sheet metal, all executed in black and white. Each sheet is prepared and worked by the artist with the same attention to detail and the same meticulous process.

With a deep passion for the contrast between light and shadow, Cortona brings this theme to the fore in a distinctive way through the chiaroscuro of this series. The use of black and white allows the artist to explore the subtle emotional nuances of every single gaze, creating a depth and intensity that captures the viewer's attention.

The result is a collection of works that explore the depths of the human soul through the powerful language of chiaroscuro.

Homage to Caravaggio and Michelangelo

Maestri di Marmo

Oil on Carrara marble

✦

With Maestri di Marmo (Masters of Marble), Cortona pays homage to two giants of art history: Caravaggio and Michelangelo.

Painted on fine Carrara marble, the works reinterpret iconic details such as The Incredulity of Saint Thomas and The Creation of Adam, turning them into a dialogue between tradition and the contemporary.

Marble, a symbol of eternity and beauty, becomes the ideal support to celebrate the expressive power of these masterpieces, offering the viewer a new perspective on their extraordinary artistic legacy.

Oil on tile

Frammenti

2020

✦

FRAMMENTI (Fragments) is a collection of oil paintings on tiles of different sizes, where each work represents an emotion, an instant, a part of the human experience.

Each tile is a fragment of a larger story: a dialogue between colour, matter and feeling that invites the viewer to recognise themselves in the infinite nuances of the human soul.

The variety of formats creates a dynamic visual rhythm, turning each work into a tile of an emotional mosaic. Together, these fragments compose an intense and personal narrative on the complexity of emotions.

Illustrations inspired by astrological glyphs

Dopo di Noi

2024

✦

.DOPO DI NOI. (After Us) is the first project born from the collaboration between two sisters and two art forms: Cortona's painting and the poetry of the writer Liè Larousse.

For the poetry collection published in 2024, Cortona created the cover and seven illustrations inspired by astrological glyphs, ancient symbols that hold universal and spiritual meanings.

Each illustration accompanies a poem, becoming a visual message that invites reflection, inner growth and the rediscovery of the bond between human beings and the universe.

  • Be What We Are

    Sacred Heart

    Limited edition print 7/20 · 100% cotton paper, handmade in Amalfi, 300 gsm, rough texture · 30 × 40 cm

    Available

    I associated the Sacred Heart with the poem “Be What We Are”, an illustration included in the poetry collection «Dopo di Noi».

    I wanted to depict the anatomical Heart because everything in us begins from a deeper Love: we are Love, Spirit and Intellect, nourished together by our inner fire, which I represented with flames in the shape of a cross, and by suffering, the veins of the heart shaped like thorns, that pain which helps us bring out who we are and what we have the right to co-create through our own talent: the emanation of our own Light, depicted by the rays of light that radiate from the center of the heart.

    The Sacred Heart is the symbol of Divine Love and Universal Compassion, a source of spiritual energy that manifests in the heart of the human being. This symbol is often associated with devotion to Jesus Christ, but it has a broader interpretation that goes beyond religious practices.

    The Sacred Heart is a call to inner searching, to opening the heart to others and to the Divine, to connecting with our own Spiritual Essence. It is an invitation to cultivate unconditional love, Gratitude and acceptance, transcending the barriers of the ego.

    Its symbolism hides deeper secrets that can guide the individual along the spiritual path, offering a doorway to understanding and Inner Illumination.

  • Only the Sun and the Earth Can

    Glyph of the Sun and the Earth

    Limited edition print 1/20 · 100% cotton paper, handmade in Amalfi, 300 gsm, rough texture · 30 × 40 cm

    Available

    I associated the Glyphs of the Sun and the Earth with the poem “Only the Sun and the Earth Can”, an illustration included in the poetry collection «Dopo di Noi».

    I wanted to depict the Earth placed at the center, as the point of the Sun's glyph, represented by a cross that stands for the glyph of the Earth, dotted and almost invisible: this is because the invisible is everywhere, and the cross, besides spirit, represents stability.

    The Sun represents our inner strength, our ego, the masculine side of us that needs to find balance and embrace our feminine side, the Earth, which in the drawing is seen at the center of everything. Their Union and their Balance are the highest manifestation of the Power of Creation.

    The Glyph of the Sun symbolizes vitality and the expression of the Self in astrology, representing a person's deepest essence and giving them the energy to shine in the world. In the esoteric world the Sun is associated with the mind and the intellect, representing the completion of the “Great Work”. In short, the Sun is a vital cosmic force, fundamental to life and with deep meanings in many spiritual and esoteric practices.

    “The Sun is the image of the Creator” (Hermetic)

    The Glyph of the Earth symbolizes space and spirituality. In Greek mythology it is personified by the goddess Gaia, mother of all living beings. The Earth embodies life, fertility and femininity, acting as a source of balance among the elements that compose it. This maternal image is reflected in the idea of a Mother Nature who generously gives her fruits to her children.

It connects us with the subtlest energies and helps us perceive reality beyond the physical plane.

    I associated the Glyph of Pluto with the poem “It Could Be Me, It Could Be You”, an illustration included in the poetry collection «Dopo di Noi».

    It represents the power to confront the repetitive and dysfunctional patterns that can hinder our path of growth, opening the way to a new awareness and a new life. Pluto is a symbol of inner strength and power that resonates with the INTRINSIC HUMAN DESIRE for CHANGE and EVOLUTION.
