Series I

Innocence

17 oils on canvas

✦

The debut series INNOCENCE I (17 oils on canvas) marks a fundamental turning point in Cortona's artistic research. Through a deep experimentation with primary colours and their visceral nuances, an intense visual and emotional metamorphosis takes shape.

From the partial framings of the early works, the artist's gaze gradually narrows until it “enters” the subjects themselves, breaking down every distance: the space on the canvas shrinks to make room for the exploration of the Soul. An inner immersion that deepens further in series II and III, turning painting into a pure journey through the emotional depth of humanity.

Series II

Innocence

Oil and photoluminescent pigments on galvanized sheet metal

✦

INNOCENCE – Series II stems from Cortona's research into inner light and from the desire to give new life to her subjects' gaze through art.

The works are handmade on galvanized sheet metal with natural photoluminescent pigments which, once charged by light, glow in the dark, revealing intense and surprising colours. Each painting is the result of a long artisanal process, in which technique and sensitivity merge to create a constantly changing visual experience.

Light becomes an integral part of the work, revealing what remains invisible and inviting the viewer to embark on a journey towards discovering their own inner light.

Series III

Innocence

10 oils on galvanized sheet metal — black and white

✦

Series III of INNOCENCE is the final chapter of the series, made up of 10 oil paintings on galvanized sheet metal, all executed in black and white. Each sheet is prepared and worked by the artist with the same attention to detail and the same meticulous process.

With a deep passion for the contrast between light and shadow, Cortona brings this theme to the fore in a distinctive way through the chiaroscuro of this series. The use of black and white allows the artist to explore the subtle emotional nuances of every single gaze, creating a depth and intensity that captures the viewer's attention.

The result is a collection of works that explore the depths of the human soul through the powerful language of chiaroscuro.

Homage to Caravaggio and Michelangelo

Maestri di Marmo

Oil on Carrara marble

✦

With Maestri di Marmo (Masters of Marble), Cortona pays homage to two giants of art history: Caravaggio and Michelangelo.

Painted on fine Carrara marble, the works reinterpret iconic details such as The Incredulity of Saint Thomas and The Creation of Adam, turning them into a dialogue between tradition and the contemporary.

Marble, a symbol of eternity and beauty, becomes the ideal support to celebrate the expressive power of these masterpieces, offering the viewer a new perspective on their extraordinary artistic legacy.

Oil on tile

Frammenti

2020

✦

FRAMMENTI (Fragments) is a collection of oil paintings on tiles of different sizes, where each work represents an emotion, an instant, a part of the human experience.

Each tile is a fragment of a larger story: a dialogue between colour, matter and feeling that invites the viewer to recognise themselves in the infinite nuances of the human soul.

The variety of formats creates a dynamic visual rhythm, turning each work into a tile of an emotional mosaic. Together, these fragments compose an intense and personal narrative on the complexity of emotions.

Illustrations inspired by astrological glyphs

Dopo di Noi

2024

✦

.DOPO DI NOI. (After Us) is the first project born from the collaboration between two sisters and two art forms: Cortona's painting and the writer's poetry.

For the poetry collection published in 2024, Cortona created the cover and seven illustrations inspired by astrological glyphs, ancient symbols that hold universal and spiritual meanings.

Each illustration accompanies a poem, becoming a visual message that invites reflection, inner growth and the rediscovery of the bond between human beings and the universe.
