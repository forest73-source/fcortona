PRIVACY & COOKIES

✦

How your data is handled when you visit this website.

Data controller

The data controller is Cortona Francesca, artist. For any question you can write to cortonaitalianpainter@gmail.com.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies: no profiling or advertising cookies and no statistics cookies. That is why no consent banner is shown.

Fonts and images are hosted directly on the site. There are no social network pixels, embedded videos or third-party maps.

Visitor statistics

To know how many people visit the site and which pages they read, I use GoatCounter, a service that does not use cookies and does not track people. It only collects anonymous, aggregated data: the page visited, the referring site (for example Instagram or Google), the browser and device type, and the country. It is not possible to identify who visited the site.

Browsing data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). Like any web server, it automatically records some technical data needed for operation and security, such as your IP address and browser type. This data is not used to identify you.

Contact form and newsletter

The contact form and the newsletter sign-up open your email program with a ready-made message. No data is sent until you send the email yourself. Your name and email address are used only to reply to you or to send you the news you asked for, and are deleted whenever you ask.

Social links

The Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest and WhatsApp buttons are plain links: nothing is loaded from those services until you click them. Once opened, each network's own privacy rules apply.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its use or object to its processing by writing to cortonaitalianpainter@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
