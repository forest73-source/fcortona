BIOGRAPHY

Art and its Essence

Cortona's artistic expression is intrinsically Spiritual in nature. Through holistic and esoteric communication, she strives to convey a deeper message through painting, urging the viewer to turn their gaze toward their own inner SELF. Her research focuses on Knowledge and Awareness of the Self and the Senses, to reconnect with one's own inner Light: for Cortona, art is Healing.

"Art that does not serve to Heal is not Art"

— Alejandro Jodorowsky

Born in Rome in 1984 and trained at Central Saint Martins in London in 2008, Francesca stands out on the contemporary scene for her innovative use of photoluminescent pigments combined with classical oil painting. These materials generate light in the dark, becoming a metaphor for inner beauty that expands. Awarded in 2021 by Vittorio Sgarbi, she has extended her research to Crypto Art, launching the NFT collection '7CHAKRA CONNECTION' for humanitarian purposes.
